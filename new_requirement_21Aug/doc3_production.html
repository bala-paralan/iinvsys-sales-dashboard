Production Head — Full Oversight

The Production Head controls everything from order receipt to POD confirmation. They see all orders, assign engineers, manage the Gantt schedule, review BOM, approve QC, and authorise dispatch. All financial values are visible to the Head. The Head is the only role that can unlock dispatch — engineers cannot self-dispatch.

  • Receives production orders triggered by Sales CO confirmation
  • Assigns engineers to orders (can split one order across engineers)
  • Reviews and approves QC results — gate before dispatch
  • Authorises all dispatches — logs courier/vehicle details
  • Tracks delivery and records POD — triggers Installation creation

Screen PD-HD-07 · Production Head
QC Approval Gate

When an engineer submits their QC results, it lands here for the Head's review. The Head reviews the test results, attached photos/documents, and any deviation notes. Only after the Head approves QC is the dispatch option unlocked. This is a mandatory gate — no engineer can bypass it.

QC Gate Is Mandatory
Engineers cannot mark an order as "dispatch ready" — only the Production Head can do that after reviewing QC results. This is enforced at the backend level. If the Head is absent, only the Head's delegate (configured by IT) can approve QC. This prevents accidental dispatch of faulty units.
Screen PD-HD-08 · Production Head
Dispatch Authorization — Head-Only Gate

After QC approval, the Production Head authorises the physical dispatch. This screen captures courier details, vehicle/AWB number, expected delivery date, and packing list. Only after the Head confirms dispatch is the tracking activated and the customer notified.

Production Engineer — My Assigned Orders Only

Each Production Engineer sees only the orders assigned to them by the Production Head. They follow the WIP steps, log progress at each step, upload photos for each completed stage, and submit QC results when done. They CANNOT see financial values, other engineers' orders, or trigger dispatch. Their job is purely to build and report progress.

  • Sees only their assigned production orders
  • Follows step-by-step WIP instructions set by Head
  • Logs progress and uploads photos at each step
  • Submits QC test results (Head must approve before dispatch)
  • Cannot see order value, revenue, or other engineers' work
