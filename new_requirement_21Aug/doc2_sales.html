Sales Director — Sales Command

The Sales Director controls the entire sales function. They create leads from CXO contacts and assign directly to any Sales Manager or Executive. They drill into any rep's deals, see per-customer activity logs across the whole team, and approve all discounts >10% and all Commercial Orders. Every screen gives them controls no other role has.

  • Creates leads/deals and assigns to any team member at any level
  • Sees all 4 managers' pipelines and all 8 execs' activity logs
  • Customer 360: full interaction history across all reps, all time
  • Approves: discounts >10%, all COs, proposal releases

Sales Manager — My Team Only

Each Sales Manager manages 2 Sales Executives within their domain. They see their 2 Execs' deals plus their own direct deals — nothing from the other 3 Sales Managers. They view per-customer activity logs for their executives, approve small discounts (3–10%), and submit proposals to the Director for larger discounts. They log their own activities daily.

  • Sees only their 2 Execs' pipeline + their own deals
  • Views per-customer activity logs for their team
  • Approves discounts 3–10% (above goes to Director)
  • Logs daily activities per customer
  • Cannot see other managers' numbers or company totals

Sales Executive — My Pipeline Only

Each Sales Executive manages their own deals within the domain assigned by their Sales Manager. They log every customer interaction per account — not just per deal. They cannot see other executives' pipelines, company-wide revenue, or their manager's deals. Their focus is entirely on their own accounts and daily activities.

  • Sees only their own deals (assigned by Manager or Director)
  • Logs calls, visits, emails, WhatsApp per customer account
  • Daily activity logging is mandatory — tracked by system
  • Self-approves discounts up to 3%; submits above to Manager
  • Creates new leads / deals and submits for Manager visibility

Why Per-Customer (not per-deal)?
A Sales Executive might have 3 different deals at DMRC Delhi — Rolling Stock monitoring, Platform safety sensors, and a software license renewal. All conversations with DMRC contacts — regardless of which deal is being discussed — appear in one unified DMRC timeline. The Director and Manager can understand the full relationship, not just deal-by-deal fragments. This is how CRMs like Salesforce work.
