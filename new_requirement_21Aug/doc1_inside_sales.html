Sales Director — Inside Sales View

The Sales Director has complete visibility into the entire Inside Sales function. They can create leads themselves from CXO meetings, trade shows, ministry contacts, or strategic accounts, and assign directly to any IS Executive or even bypass IS and put the lead straight into the Sales pipeline as a hot prospect. They see every exec's activity log, every customer interaction, and full team analytics.

  • Creates and assigns leads to any team member
  • Views all IS Exec's leads and per-customer activity logs
  • Sees team-level and company-level KPIs
  • Can approve or override handoffs to Sales

Screen IS-DIR-03 · Sales Director
Director Lead Capture & Assignment

1
Bypass IS — Critical Feature
When the Sales Director meets a CXO at a Railways board meeting, that lead does not need IS nurturing — it's already warm. The "Bypass IS → Assign to Sales Executive" option creates the lead AND opens a Prospect-stage deal in SPENCO simultaneously. The Sales Exec gets everything pre-filled.
2
Director Managed Hold
Sometimes the Director wants to make the first call personally before handing off. "Director Managed" keeps the lead in a special Director queue — it doesn't disappear into an exec's list. Once the Director logs initial contact, they can reassign with context already recorded.

IS Head — Inside Sales Manager

The IS Head manages all IS Executives directly. They assign and reassign leads, review each exec's activity logs per customer, approve handoffs to the Sales team, and track team performance. They CANNOT see Sales pipeline data, revenue figures, or other Sales Executives' deals.

  • Assigns and reassigns leads among their IS Executives
  • Views per-customer activity logs for each exec
  • Approves or rejects Sales handoff requests
  • Sets daily/weekly targets per exec
IS HEAD
Screen IS-HD-01 · IS Head
IS Head Dashboard

The IS Head sees their team — all IS Executives, their lead counts, activity stats, and today's priority actions. They do NOT see revenue, Sales pipeline, or data from other departments.

IS Executive / SDR — My Work Only

The IS Executive sees only their own assigned leads. They log every customer interaction (calls, emails, visits, WhatsApp) against the customer record — not just the deal. They run BANT qualification and request handoff when a lead is ready for Sales. They CANNOT see other execs' leads, team KPIs, or Sales pipeline data.

  • Every interaction logged per customer (not just per lead)
  • Tasks auto-generated from activity outcomes
  • BANT progress tracked in real time
  • Personal targets visible — no peer comparison shown
IS EXECUTIVE
Screen IS-EX-01 · IS Executive
My Dashboard — My Leads, My Tasks, My Targets

The IS Executive's personal command center. Shows only their own leads, their tasks due today (auto-created from logged activity outcomes), their personal target progress, and their recent activity log. No team data, no other exec's numbers.

Per-Customer Activity Log — Not Per-Lead
Activities are logged against the COMPANY (BHEL Trichy) — not just this specific lead. If the exec has 2 leads at BHEL Trichy, both appear in one unified timeline. When the exec selects "Next Task" from the dropdown and saves the activity, the system auto-creates a task with a due date and adds it to their dashboard. No manual task creation needed — every activity automatically seeds the next action.
