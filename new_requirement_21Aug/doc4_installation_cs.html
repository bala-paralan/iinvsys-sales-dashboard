Installation Head — All Jobs, All Engineers

The Installation Head receives a new INS-XXXX job automatically when Production confirms POD. They assign a Field Engineer, monitor installation progress, approve the customer sign-off, and track CSAT scores. They also have a read-only view of the CS team's SLA performance — so they know if a customer is having support issues on a newly installed product.

  • Receives auto-created installation jobs on POD confirmation
  • Assigns and reassigns Field Engineers to jobs
  • Reviews installation progress in real time
  • Approves customer sign-off and closes the installation

CS Manager — All Tickets, All Agents, SLA Control

The CS Manager runs the customer support team. They see all tickets from all CS Agents, monitor SLA countdown timers, compare agent performance, track AMC renewals, and push renewal leads back to Sales. They are the ONLY role that sees agent-to-agent comparison data.

  • Sees all tickets from all agents (cross-agent visibility)
  • Manages SLA timers and escalates breaching tickets
  • Views agent performance comparison — exclusive to CS Manager
  • Tracks AMC/Warranty and triggers renewal leads to Sales
  • Generates CS analytics and monthly service reports

AMC Renewal Loop to Sales
When the CS Manager clicks "Push to Sales as Suspect", the system creates a new SPENCO deal at Suspect stage for the relevant Sales Executive (the one who originally closed the deal). This is the full loop: Installed → AMC → Renewal → back into SPENCO. No manual data entry — the account details, product, and contact all pre-fill.

Field Engineer — My Assigned Jobs Only

The Field Engineer sees only their own assigned installation jobs. They conduct the site survey, follow the installation steps, upload photos at each stage, and collect the customer's digital sign-off and CSAT score on a tablet. They cannot see other engineers' jobs, financial data, or CS tickets.

  • Sees only their own assigned installation jobs
  • Conducts site survey and logs pre-installation conditions
  • Follows step-by-step installation checklist
  • Collects digital sign-off and CSAT on-site (tablet-optimised)

CS Agent — My Tickets Only

Each CS Agent sees only their own assigned tickets. They work each ticket, log activity (calls, emails, remote access sessions), update ticket status, and close tickets when resolved. They CANNOT see other agents' tickets, team SLA comparisons, AMC contract values, or renewal pipeline.

  • Sees only their own assigned tickets
  • Logs all activity per ticket (calls, emails, sessions)
  • Updates ticket status and resolution details
  • Cannot see other agents' work or team performance
